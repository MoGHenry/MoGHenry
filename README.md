## 🤖 AI Software Engineer
### *Architect of the AI Era, not its replacement*

I'm not a coder being replaced by AI — I'm an architect commanding an army of AI agents.

In an era where AI is fundamentally reshaping software development, I believe the next generation of engineers must master **two seemingly opposing capabilities**:

- 🔧 **Deep technical foundation** — understanding systems from the bottom up, not just stitching together AI-generated snippets
- 🤝 **Effective human-AI collaboration** — knowing how to direct, constrain, and amplify AI's output

### 🎯 My Philosophy

> *"The best AI SWE isn't the one who writes the most code, nor the one who lets AI write everything — but the one who knows **when to set AI free** and **when to rein it in**."*

| Principle | What it means in practice |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| 🧠 **Think like an architect, execute with AI** | I design the system. AI helps implement it. |
| ⚖️ **Balance autonomy and constraint** | Free rein on boilerplate. Tight constraints on critical paths, security, and architecture. |
| 🛡️ **Guard against AI's pitfalls** | Hallucinations, subtle bugs, security blind spots, and silent technical debt all demand human judgment. |
| 🚀 **Multiply, don't replace** | AI is my force multiplier — it scales my impact, it doesn't substitute my thinking. |

### 🛠️ How I Work With AI

- ✅ Let AI handle: scaffolding, refactoring, test generation, documentation, exploration of unfamiliar APIs
- 🚫 I personally own: system design, security boundaries, data models, performance-critical paths, and final review
- 🔍 Always verify: I don't ship what I can't read, explain, and defend

### 👀 Interests & Focus

- Computer Science · AI-Assisted Software Engineering
- Currently sharpening: **Python**, **Web Development**, **Data Analytics**

## 🤖 AI 软件工程师
### *AI 时代的架构师，而非被替代者*

我不是被 AI 替代的码农，而是**指挥 AI 大军的架构师**。

在 AI 重塑软件开发的时代，下一代工程师需要同时掌握**两种看似矛盾的能力**：

- 🔧 **扎实的底层技术功底** —— 理解系统的本质，而不是仅仅拼接 AI 生成的代码片段
- 🤝 **高效的人机协同能力** —— 懂得如何指挥 AI、约束 AI、放大 AI 的产出

### 🎯 我的理念

> *"最优秀的 AI 工程师，不是写代码最多的人，也不是把一切交给 AI 的人——而是知道**什么时候让 AI 自由发挥**，**什么时候对 AI 严格约束**的人。"*

| 原则 | 在实践中意味着什么 |
|------|------------------|
| 🧠 **像架构师一样思考，借助 AI 高效执行** | 我设计系统，AI 协助实现 |
| ⚖️ **平衡自由与约束** | 模板代码可以放手让 AI 写；关键路径、安全、架构必须严控 |
| 🛡️ **警惕 AI 的负面影响** | 幻觉、隐蔽的 Bug、安全盲点、悄然累积的技术债，都需要人的判断 |
| 🚀 **放大而非替代** | AI 是我的力量倍增器——它扩展我的影响力，而不是替代我的思考 |

### 🛠️ 我如何与 AI 协作

- ✅ 交给 AI：搭建脚手架、重构、生成测试、撰写文档、探索陌生 API
- 🚫 我亲自把控：系统设计、安全边界、数据模型、性能关键路径、最终评审
- 🔍 始终验证：**看不懂、讲不清、扛不住的代码，绝不上线**
